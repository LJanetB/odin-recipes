Banana Cake's Recipe

Description

This is a delicious banana cake recipe that is perfect for any occasion. It's moist, flavorful, and easy to make.

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe bananas
  • 1/3 cup melted butter
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • Pinch of salt
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 large egg, beaten
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. In a mixing bowl, mash the ripe bananas.
  3. Add the melted butter to the mashed bananas and mix well.
  4. Mix in the baking soda and salt.
  5. Stir in the sugar, beaten egg, and vanilla extract.
  6. Gradually add the flour and mix until just combined.
  7. Pour the batter into a greased loaf pan.
  8. Bake for 60 minutes or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  9. Let the cake cool before serving.
